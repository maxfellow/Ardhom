Our approach

Earth before cement

The mix is mostly sieved laterite soil, stabilised with a small share of cement. Not the other way round.

Engineered to interlock

A tongue on top, a groove below. Courses align themselves, so walls rise straighter with only a thin bond.

Material intelligence

Soil is checked and sieved, pressed into a dense block, then cured slowly in shade before it leaves the yard.

Detail as discipline

Corners, ends and openings get their own blocks, so the finished wall needs no patching and no plaster to hide behind.
